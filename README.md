# 澎科大無人機物流與風險場展示

澎科大 drone logistics + wind-danger flight-safety twin demo: a browser-only
Penghu route planner, synthetic wind-danger field, and scheduled drone animation.
The wind scenario, safety thresholds and proposed air corridors are demonstration
assumptions, not measured weather, approved flight corridors or operational advice.

Open [the integrated demo](penghu-app/daily.html). This repository contains generated public runtime
files built from the private penghu-drone and DT-Tourism source repositories.
The source build is `python scripts/build_static.py --viewer-root <DT checkout>
--node-modules <installed dependencies>`. `BUILD_INFO.json` records revisions and
dependency versions. No application server, API key or live data service is needed.
Publish this entire directory, including `.nojekyll`, as the GitHub Pages source.
The same files work at the domain root or under any project subdirectory.

Open [今日配送計畫](penghu-app/daily.html) for the baseline vaccine delivery day:
one mock incoming batch, two health-center orders, one drone and two vans. The
day is 2027-01-13, 06:00–18:00 (Asia/Taipei): 冬季東北季風日（依中央氣象署測站冬季統計校準的合成情境，非單日實測）.
It is a synthetic winter north-east monsoon day calibrated to published wind
statistics, not a recording of one real day and not a forecast. The mock vaccine
batch is 200 doses released at 07:00; the baseline orders are 80 doses to Magong
(deadline 11:00) and 40 doses to Qimei (deadline 09:30). The release time, doses,
deadlines and receiving windows are demonstration assumptions, not a real clinic
schedule. The panel-core panels, three map actors and replay controls share the same computed
simulation. V-01 and V-02 have locally bundled 3D van models with wheels driven
by simulated travel distance; their 5 m display length is illustrative. Stock,
timed transfers, custody, temperature and resource energy are
calculated from inspectable mock inputs. The day summary accounts for remaining
stock and final vehicle states; the drone remains on Qimei and recovery is outside
this scenario. The hazard layer, map camera controls and route-trial panel are
available in the same workspace. Route trials use a separately labelled preview
with the original animated drone, close tail view, automatic route fitting and
flight replay on the shared day clock. The header opens 規劃, 監控 and 風險.
規劃 edits orders/vehicles, proposes a plan, shows the independent engine verdict and
lets a person adopt it. 監控 plays adopted runs, or the original baseline when none
exists. 風險 changes the shared scenario, replans trials and reverifies adoption.
Settings can connect an OpenAI-compatible model; every output identifies live model,
recorded replay or non-AI rules. Five recordings of real `gpt-6.1-sol` model calls
(high reasoning effort, recorded 2026-10-05, outside any sandbox, against the winter
scenario above) are bundled and are used for the scripted steps. Any other schedule
input uses the visibly labelled rule fallback (規則排程（非 AI）) unless the visitor
connects a model. Free-form sentences need a connected live model: a risk sentence
without a recording shows a message and leaves the scenario unchanged, while the
example buttons keep working. The AI only proposes: the deterministic engine in the
browser verifies every proposal, and a person adopts it. No model URL or key is
preconfigured, and no key is in the build.

## Data sources and attribution

- **OpenStreetMap:** coastline/island masks, roads, buildings and named facility
  locations in the graph and site meshes. © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  [Copyright and attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright);
  [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
  OSM-derived database contents remain subject to ODbL attribution and share-alike
  requirements. Rendered meshes are derived from these data; building heights
  without explicit OSM height tags use an assumed 4 m. Air links are authored
  demo relationships, not OSM flight permissions. Private build receipts retain
  the Overpass snapshots, hashes and per-output lineage.
- **Terrain:** 內政部地政司，2025年版全臺灣20公尺網格數值地形模型DTM資料，
  澎湖 20 m DTM. [Official dataset](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/176927).
  此開放資料依[政府資料開放授權條款－第1版（OGDL 1.0）](https://data.gov.tw/license)
  進行公眾釋出，使用者於遵守本條款各項規定之前提下，得利用之。
  The demo resamples this source and adds an OSM coast mask, sea plane and coast
  skirts; missing/nonpositive land elevations have an assumed 0.1 m floor.
- **Danger frames and router field:** generated by the private Python twin from
  a synthetic winter monsoon scenario whose parameters are calibrated to the wind
  statistics listed under Weather and aircraft references below, with assumed
  terrain wind factors and OSM-derived land masks. The output is a simulation, not
  a meteorological feed. The calibration uses a 10 m winter mean wind of 10.9 m/s
  (mean of five offshore stations) from 25°, a daily half-range of 0.5 m/s and a
  15:00–18:00 surge configured at +5 m/s with 2 h ramps. The effective peak of
  that surge is about +3.75 m/s (at 16:30), because the two 2 h ramps overlap
  inside the 3 h window. The share of flyable sea cells falls from about 55% at
  08:00 to 32% at 12:00 and 0% at 16:30.
- **Drone model:** `data/models/CesiumDrone.glb`, from
  [Cesium SampleData](https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium/tree/main/Apps/SampleData/models/CesiumDrone).
  Copyright 2017, 2018 Analytical Graphics, Inc.; mesh and textures by Ed Mackey.
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as recorded inside
  the GLB. The model bytes are unchanged; the app changes display scale, orientation
  and position. Keep this credit, source and license link when redistributing.
- **Van model:** `data/models/kenney-van/van.glb` and its local
  `Textures/colormap.png`, from [Kenney Car Kit 3.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit),
  [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  The unmodified source asset, license and SHA-256 provenance are bundled in
  that directory. The runtime uses a representative 5 m length and animated
  wheel rotations; the appearance does not establish refrigeration capability.

**Reference aircraft.** The reference aircraft is the JEDSY Jedsy X, taken from its
public specification [S5][S6]: operating wind limit 12 m/s (compared with the 10 m
wind), body limit 15 m/s, payload 3 kg, range 120 km and endurance 118 min less a
10% reserve. The model cruises at 27.8 m/s (100 km/h, as reported in the press [S7];
the manufacturer lists 30 m/s). The manufacturer does not publish the battery
capacity, so the model derives it from the published range. Identifying the Jedsy X
as the aircraft used in 極現科技's 2026-09-07 demonstration at the 澎科大 campus is
an inference from press reports [S7][S8][S9]; those reports do not name the model
and the operator has not confirmed it. The reported scenario was medical-supply
transport from Magong to Jibei Island [S7]. This project does not claim any measured
wind margin for that demonstration.

attribution and licence text follow as the app carries it:
